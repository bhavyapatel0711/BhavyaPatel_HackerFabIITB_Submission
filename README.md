## Device Concept

The proposed device is an aluminium-gate MOS capacitor consisting of:

- **Top electrode:** Aluminium
- **Dielectric:** Silicon dioxide (SiO₂)
- **Substrate:** Lightly p-type-doped silicon
- **Backside contact:** Proposed aluminium contact

The MOS capacitor is intended as a learning-oriented process-monitor structure for exploring semiconductor fabrication and, eventually, electrical characterisation.

## Scope and Limitations

This submission documents a **proposed fabrication process and its representation in FabuBlox**. It does not claim that the physical device has been fabricated or experimentally validated.

Specifically:

- No physical fabrication has been completed.
- No annealing process is claimed as completed.
- No electrical characterisation or capacitance–voltage (C–V) measurement has been performed.
- No leakage measurements or device-performance results are claimed.
- The final FabuBlox simulation view displayed **“No notices”** at the time of capture. This is evidence of the displayed simulation state, not proof that the process is experimentally validated.

The availability and suitability of fabrication equipment, process conditions, and measurement instruments must be confirmed with the HackerFab IITB team before any physical execution.
